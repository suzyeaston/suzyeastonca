*Meanwhile / Sunday, November 22, 2026 / Temporary Coordinates, Recalculated*

Sunday morning, I woke up and watched football.

That's it. That's the headline. lol.

I haven't done that in months, not since I was staying at Wil's sublet. Wil was my manager at HMV Canada back in the 2007–2012 universe, where I specialized in jazz and classical music sales, as well as pop specialty and world music.

It was a special job. I became friends with musicians like the late, great [Paul Plimley](https://paulplimley.com/about-paul/), talked jazz with [Ross Taggart](https://www.fredstride.com/articles--essays--thoughts/ross-taggart-1967-2013/ross-taggart-1967-2013), had Tom Waits drop by, and accidentally met Selena Gomez upstairs.

Part record store, part accidental conservatory, part celebrity glitch.

And almost twenty years later, that connection gave me somewhere to land.

## previously, on the relocation simulator

In April, I got laid off and cried my ass off. By May, I'd landed another role in AI strategy and engineering.

Then July happened.

**Laid off. Again.**

Two layoffs in four months. Incredible performance from the corporate restructuring department.

Meanwhile, my living situation had become its own experimental software project. An East Van basement with flooding problems, a brief Kitsilano chapter, and a rather spectacular amount of downsizing.

This was not a cute digital-nomad era. I was trying to stay in my own city.

<!-- PHOTO: 2026-11-suitcases.jpg | Two black rolling suitcases and other bags beside a Vancouver sidewalk. | My touring setup got a little too literal. -->

October brought something beautiful: a [Basecamp artist residency](https://basecampyvr.ca/) at [HI Vancouver Jericho Beach](https://www.hihostels.ca/en/hostels/hi-vancouver-jericho-beach), where I reconnected with music and performed my first solo electronic set at [Futureproof](https://www.futureproof.website/speakers/suzy-easton/).

The musician and the engineer finally got to play the same gig.

I wrote about it in [*The Toaster Was a Decoy*](https://www.suzyeaston.ca/2026/11/02/the-toaster-was-a-decoy/) and [*An Orchestra in 8GB*](https://www.suzyeaston.ca/2026/11/02/an-orchestra-in-8gb/).

Unfortunately, HI unexpectedly paused Basecamp at the end of October, although I'd hoped the residency could continue longer. From what I later heard, it was caught up in an administrative transition and the hostel's lease situation. The program may yet return, but my stay ended.

Even the place where I'd finally caught my breath had a checkout date.

But the creative momentum survived. I made a [film inside my own simulated Vancouver](https://www.suzyeaston.ca/2026/11/13/make-art-not-war-november-2026-bc-ai-film-club-prompt-challenge/), built an a cappella-style app called [MOSAIC](https://www.suzyeaston.ca/mosaic/), and started imagining what comes next.

More music, visuals, strange little machines, and live performances. I'll share the experiments online as they evolve.

**Futureproof was just the beginning.** ♡

## new coordinates

After November's Airbnb relay race, something finally shifted.

**November 16: I got the platform engineering job.**

I start tomorrow. **HOLY SHIT.**

<!-- PHOTO: 2026-11-wake-up.jpg | Kicking Horse Coffee billboard over an East Vancouver street reading Wake Up Kick Ass. | The city has a motivational department. -->

After leaving the South Vancouver Airbnb, I made one more stop in Strathcona. After checking in, I went exploring and stumbled upon [Grandview's oldest house](https://www.heritagesitefinder.ca/location/1350-graveley-st-vancouver-bc). Built around 1900, relocated twice.

Respect, little house. Same.

<!-- PHOTO: 2026-11-grandview-house.jpg | White house with a steep staircase and heritage plaque at 1350 Graveley Street, Vancouver. | A fellow veteran of temporary coordinates. -->

Friday, I moved into a lovely room around **Gastown/Chinatown**, an area I've lived in before. A desk, a window, room to unpack. Actual coordinates.

<!-- PHOTO: 2026-11-new-room.jpg | Bright compact furnished room with a bed by a window and a desk along the opposite wall. | New headquarters. Bed, desk, no checkout time. -->

## tiny cup, enormous Sunday

I can't make coffee at home yet. I literally moved in on Friday, lol.

So this morning I went to [Prototype](https://www.prototypecoffee.ca/) on Pender and got coffee in a tiny paper cup. Decided it would be my last visit, then remembered they host [tech meetups](https://www.meetup.com/core-coffee-a-catch-up-for-ios-and-macos-developers/).

See you again, tiny cup.

Tonight the Canucks are playing Chicago. Tomorrow I start a new job. There are still bills, unknowns, and a whole lot of life to figure out.

But for the first time in months, Sunday didn't have to be a housing strategy.

<!-- PHOTO: 2026-11-canucks-chicago.jpg | TV showing the Vancouver Canucks playing Chicago on the ice during Sunday's hockey game. | Sunday night programming. No spreadsheet required. -->

Maybe presence isn't some grand revelation. Sometimes it's just football in the morning, hockey at night, and knowing where you'll wake up tomorrow.

Vancouver. Still home.

**No checkout tomorrow.**
